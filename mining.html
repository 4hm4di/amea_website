Overview

Exploration and mining

AMEA explores, extracts and plans to process solid minerals essential to global industries. Geological surveying and modern exploration identify high-potential sites, and extraction follows strict safety and environmental standards.

Our technical programme is led by formally appointed consultant geologists.

Our process

From exploration to market

Every phase is handled by qualified people, from the first survey to the buyer.

  1. Step 1

    Survey and mapping

    Geological surveys and mapping identify high-potential ground.

  2. Step 2

    Exploration

    Trenching, sampling and drilling confirm mineral occurrences.

  3. Step 3

    Estimation and feasibility

    Consultant geologists quantify resources and test viability.

  4. Step 4

    Licence conversion

    Exploration Licences are converted into Mining Leases.

  5. Step 5

    Extraction

    Responsible extraction under strict safety and environmental standards.

  6. Step 6 (planned)

    Value addition

    Refining and processing in Nigeria into higher-value products.

  7. Step 7 (planned)

    Market

    Supply to industries at home and abroad through export partnerships.
