Our background

AMEA is an indigenous, medium-sized integrated mining company founded by experienced industry professionals. We extract and process precious metals and stones, and develop agricultural ventures that complement our mining operations.

Our vision

To be a leading sustainable mining company that delivers superior value to stakeholders while fostering environmental stewardship and social responsibility.

Our mission

To lead Nigeria’s transition to resilient, innovative mining and agricultural industries for export, improving productivity and adding value to supply chains.

Strategic goals

  1. Developing a diversified portfolio of high-quality resource assets
  2. Maximising shareholder returns through value-added exploration and processing
  3. Reducing Nigeria’s dependency on oil by strengthening the mining and agriculture sectors
  4. Building a global supply chain for critical minerals and agro-products
